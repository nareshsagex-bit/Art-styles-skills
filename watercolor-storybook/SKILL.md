# Watercolor Storybook

## What it looks like
- Transparent washes of colour with soft, bleeding edges
- Visible cold-press paper grain and pigment pooling at the edges of shapes
- Loose, light pencil or ink linework under the paint
- Lots of white paper left showing, so it feels airy rather than filled-in
- Warm, gentle, friendly mood, like a picture-book spread

## Prompt template
```
[SUBJECT], watercolor children's book illustration, soft transparent washes,
bleeding edges, pigment pooling, light pencil linework, cold-press paper texture,
white space, gentle warm palette, whimsical storybook mood
```

## Must include
- `watercolor`, `children's book illustration`
- `paper texture` and `bleeding edges`
- `white space`, which stops it looking like a digital painting

## Avoid (negative prompt)
`3d, cgi, hard edges, heavy outlines, photo, oversaturated, digital airbrush`

## Palette tips
Soft cerulean sky, warm yellow ochre, peach and sage green, with a few darker accents for the eyes and hair. Let the colours stay light and translucent.

## Keeping a character consistent
Keep the character description and pose identical. In watercolor, colour identifiers (yellow jacket, white dog) carry identity more than fine detail does.

## Example
```
A girl with curly dark hair, yellow jacket and blue jeans, arms raised,
jumping happily with a white fluffy dog, watercolor children's book illustration,
soft washes, bleeding edges, light pencil lines, puffy watercolor clouds,
pale yellow sun, cold-press paper texture, lots of white space
```
