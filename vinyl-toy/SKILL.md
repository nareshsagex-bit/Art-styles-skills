# Designer Vinyl Toy

## What it looks like
- Chibi proportions: oversized head, small body, short limbs
- Smooth, glossy or semi-matte plastic with soft specular highlights
- Simplified face: dot or bead eyes, tiny or no nose, small mouth
- Shot like a product photo: seamless pastel backdrop, studio softbox lighting
- Sometimes shown with its packaging box beside it

## Prompt template
```
[SUBJECT] as a designer vinyl toy, chibi proportions, big head small body,
glossy plastic finish, smooth surfaces, cute simplified face,
studio product photography, softbox lighting, seamless pastel backdrop,
[OPTIONAL: standing next to its collectible packaging box]
```

## Must include
- `designer vinyl toy` or `blind box figure`
- `chibi proportions`
- `product photography` and `softbox lighting`

## Avoid (negative prompt)
`realistic proportions, skin pores, fabric texture, gritty, dark lighting, painterly`

## Palette tips
Pastel backdrop (mint, peach, lilac) with saturated toy colours on top. Keep it to 4–6 main colours, as with real toy paint.

## Keeping a character consistent
Keep hair, outfit and colours identical across images. Only the proportions change to chibi. Name accessories explicitly (the dog becomes a "small vinyl dog figure").

## Example
```
A girl with curly dark hair, yellow jacket and blue jeans, arms raised,
with a small white fluffy dog, designer vinyl toy, chibi proportions,
glossy plastic finish, cute simplified face, studio product photography,
softbox lighting, seamless sky-blue backdrop, collectible box beside her
```
