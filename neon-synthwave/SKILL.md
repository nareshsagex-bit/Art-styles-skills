# Neon / Synthwave

## What it looks like
- Subject drawn as glowing neon tube outlines or rim-lit silhouettes
- Dark navy or purple-black background
- A retro perspective grid floor fading to the horizon
- A big striped sunset sun, often with horizontal cuts across it
- Bloom/glow, light haze, optional chrome text or scan lines

## Prompt template
```
[SUBJECT], neon synthwave style, glowing neon outlines, magenta and cyan,
dark purple night background, retro perspective grid floor,
striped sunset sun on the horizon, bloom glow, 1980s retrowave aesthetic
```

## Must include
- `neon`, `synthwave` or `retrowave`, `glowing outlines`
- The grid floor and striped sun, the instantly recognisable motifs
- `dark background`, without which the neon can't glow

## Avoid (negative prompt)
`daylight, pastel, flat lighting, muted colours, realistic photo, watercolor`

## Palette tips
Hot magenta (#FF2A6D), electric cyan (#05D9E8) and deep purple (#2D0B59), with a sunset gradient from yellow through orange to pink.

## Keeping a character consistent
Keep the pose and silhouette identical. Clothing colours may shift to neon, so keep one anchor (e.g. "yellow neon jacket") for recognisability.

## Example
```
A girl with curly hair, jacket and jeans, arms raised, jumping with a fluffy dog,
neon synthwave style, glowing magenta and cyan outlines, dark purple night sky,
retro perspective grid floor, striped orange-pink sunset sun on the horizon,
bloom glow, 1980s retrowave aesthetic
```
