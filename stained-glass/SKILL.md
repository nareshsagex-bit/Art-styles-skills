# Stained Glass

## What it looks like
- The image is cut into glass pieces separated by thick dark lead lines (called leading)
- Translucent, saturated jewel colours that glow as if lit from behind
- Slight colour variation and bubbles inside each glass piece
- Simplified shapes, because every area must be a cuttable piece
- Often framed by an arch or a decorative border

## Prompt template
```
[SUBJECT] as a stained glass window, thick black lead lines, translucent jewel-tone
glass, backlit glow, textured glass with subtle bubbles, simplified shapes,
decorative border, [ARCH/ROUND/RECTANGULAR] window frame
```

## Must include
- `stained glass window`, `lead lines` (or `leading`)
- `backlit` or `light shining through`, which creates the glow
- `simplified shapes`, which keeps faces and hands readable

## Avoid (negative prompt)
`photo, 3d render, soft gradients, thin lines, realistic skin, blurry`

## Palette tips
Cobalt blue, ruby red, emerald green and amber yellow, with white or clear glass for highlights and the sun.

## Keeping a character consistent
Use strong colour identifiers (yellow jacket, blue jeans, white dog) because the glass pieces flatten detail. Keep the pose large and clear.

## Example
```
A girl with curly dark hair, yellow jacket and blue jeans, arms raised, jumping
with a white fluffy dog, stained glass window, thick black lead lines,
translucent jewel-tone glass, glowing amber sun, cobalt blue sky pieces,
white glass clouds, backlit, arched window frame with decorative border
```
